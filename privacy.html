Privacy Policy

Dead End Haul · Effective October 19, 2026

The short version
  • We only use your Discord user ID and display name, so you can play and rejoin a game you dropped out of.
  • Other players in your session see your display name and what you do in the game.
  • We don't run ads, use analytics or trackers, sell your data, or keep a database of players.
  • Session data lives in the game server's memory and is gone within 12 hours (sooner if the server restarts).

This policy explains what information Dead End Haul (the "Game") handles when you play it as a Discord Activity, and why. Dead End Haul is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email deadendhaul@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

From your Discord account. When you launch the Game, Discord asks you to authorize it with the identify permission. That lets the Game's server ask Discord for your basic account profile. The profile includes your user ID, username, display name, avatar and a few account settings. Of that, we only use your user ID and display name, and we ignore the rest. We do not get your email address, your messages, your server list or your friends list.

Session information. We use the ID of the Discord activity session (the voice channel's instance) to put everyone in that channel into the same lobby. During a game, the server passes gameplay messages between players, for example movement, shots, items and votes. It does not store them.

Technical information. Like any website, the Game's server and the services that carry its traffic receive your IP address and basic connection details when you connect. Those services are Discord's activity proxy and our hosting provider. We do not log or store IP addresses ourselves. Our hosting provider may keep standard network logs under its own policies.

On your device. The Game saves your settings (volume, display options), your character's look, and a backup copy of your in-game backpack in your browser's storage on your device, so a dropped connection doesn't cost you your gear. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and put you in the right lobby.
  • To show your display name to the other players in your session.
  • To recognize you if your connection drops, so you get your survivor and backpack back when you rejoin.
  • To keep the Game running and fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any automated decisions about you.

3. Who sees it

  • Other players in your session see your display name, your character and your in-game actions. The player who hosts the session runs the game on their device, so gameplay information about the session (including display names) passes through it while you play.
  • Discord runs the activity platform and its proxy. Discord's own Privacy Policy covers what Discord does with your information.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server the Game connects to, and only processes data to provide that service.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share something, or if it's needed to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

We don't keep a player database. Your user ID and display name are held in the server's memory for up to 12 hours after you launch the Game, so you can rejoin a game in progress. They are deleted after that or whenever the server restarts. Lobbies and games exist only while they are being played. The server's own log records only anonymous session and player numbers, not names or Discord IDs. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it.

5. Your choices

  • Stop sharing your Discord info: in Discord, go to User Settings → Authorized Apps and remove Dead End Haul. The Game will ask again if you launch it later.
  • Clear data on your device: clear the site data for the activity in your browser, or Discord's cache in the desktop app.
  • Ask us about your data: email deadendhaul@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted. Because we store almost nothing, the answer is usually "nothing beyond the current session", but we will always reply. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data. We will honor those requests.

6. Children

The Game is for players aged 13 and over, which matches Discord's own minimum age in most countries (players must also meet the minimum age where they live). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, email us and we will delete anything we hold about them.

7. Security

Connections to the Game are encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets) through Discord's proxy. We keep only what's needed, for as short a time as possible. No system is perfectly secure, but holding almost no data is the best protection we can offer.

8. Where it's processed

The Game's server is operated from the United States. If you play from elsewhere, your connection and the session information described above are processed there.

9. Changes

If we change how the Game handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, for example if we start storing something new, we'll also announce it in the Game.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: deadendhaul@gmail.com.
